The meeting is the work

A discovery meeting and an on-site working session keep the same standard at two lengths. Both end with a spec.

A discovery meeting is for naming the need. You talk. He writes. You leave with a page, not a stack of impressions.

An on-site working session holds that practice for longer. He stays while the hard parts get named, including the parts the room refuses. Use it when one sitting will not surface the constraint.

He will travel a long distance for this. The standard does not drop because the trip was long.
