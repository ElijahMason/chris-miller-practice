The work I take

Dear reader,

I will come for a discovery meeting. I will write a spec in plain language. I will implement what that spec says. I will stay for an on-site working session when the room needs more than one sitting.

These are separate pieces of work. A meeting is not a build, and a build is not a guess about what the meeting meant.

A long trip is acceptable when the problem is in the room. The meeting stays part of the engineering either way.

Yours,

Chris Miller
