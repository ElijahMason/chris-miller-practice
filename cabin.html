The meeting is the work

A discovery meeting and an on-site working session keep the same standard. Both end with a spec.

In a discovery meeting you talk and he writes. You leave with a page, not a stack of impressions.

An on-site working session holds that practice for longer. He stays while the hard parts get named, including the parts the room refuses. He will travel a long distance for this. The standard does not drop because the trip was long.
