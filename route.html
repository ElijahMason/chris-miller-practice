Route

The route of an engagement

An engagement runs from the room to the handoff. The spec is written before the build. Refusals sit beside decisions, in the same plain language.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Chris Miller comes to the room where the work happens. A long trip is acceptable when the problem is there and will not come to him.

  2. 02

    Listen

    He sits with the people who hold the constraint. The meeting is part of the engineering. He writes while they talk, so the sentences stay theirs.

  3. 03

    Spec

    The spec is plain language. It records what was decided and what was refused. An open point stays open, with the name of the person who can close it.

  4. 04

    Build

    He implements from that page. When a decision changes, the page changes before the system does. The build does not run ahead of the writing.

  5. 05

    Handoff

    He walks the system with you against the spec. You keep the system and the page. The next person can see what the room refused.
